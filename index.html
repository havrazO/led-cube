LED Cube: From Bits to AI

You build your own 4 × 4 × 4 LED cube and program it, on a journey through the history of computing: bits, assembly, C, the web, and AI. At the end, four cubes play one show together.

Before the first session

Set up the Arduino IDE on your MacBook.

Download

Download all files (ZIP): all pages released so far, to read offline (the firmware folder is added with Session 4). From Session 4 on you need it: download it while you are still on the school Wi-Fi, before you join CUBE-LAB.
